# AI Use in Course Materials

This course makes transparent and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in the development of selected teaching materials.

AI systems, including large language models (LLMs) and code-assist tools, may be used to support the preparation of:

- Explanatory text and structured notes
- Worked examples
- Example code snippets
- Practice questions
- Draft outlines and lesson structure
- Content refinement and clarity improvements

AI tools are used as **assistive technologies**, not as authoritative sources.

---

## How AI Is Used

Where AI has assisted in the creation of a page, a notice appears at the bottom of that page outlining:

- The role AI played (e.g., drafting, outlining, code example generation)
- The tools used
- Whether the material was human-reviewed

This approach models transparent and accountable AI use consistent with contemporary academic and professional standards.

---

## Human Review and Academic Responsibility

All AI-assisted content is:

- Reviewed and edited by the lecturer
- Evaluated for alignment with module learning outcomes
- Checked for technical accuracy
- Tested where code examples are provided

AI systems can generate plausible but incorrect outputs. For this reason, AI outputs are critically assessed before publication.

Final responsibility for all published material rests with the lecturer.

---

## Why Disclose AI Use?

As a course in IT and Computing, this module recognises that AI tools are increasingly integrated into professional practice. Transparent disclosure serves several purposes:

- It models ethical and responsible AI use
- It distinguishes assistive use from authorship
- It reinforces critical evaluation of AI-generated content
- It aligns with institutional guidance on AI transparency

Students are encouraged to adopt similar standards of clarity and accountability when using AI tools in their own work, in accordance with college policy.

---

## Questions About AI Use

If you have any questions about how AI has been used in this course, please ask.

Transparency supports trust, critical thinking, and professional integrity.
